About

Rev. Christine L Wines

Founder & Ordained Minister

Healthy spirituality, practiced with love

Rev. Christine L Wines founded Loving Spirit Healing & Ceremonies on a simple belief: that healthy spirituality comes from love, compassion, and honoring each person's own path. As an ordained minister and holistic health practitioner, she walks with people through life's most meaningful moments — from the joy of a wedding or a child's blessing, to the sorrow of a farewell, to the quiet work of healing and clearing.

Every ceremony, altar, ritual, and reading is personalized. Nothing is one-size-fits-all; each is shaped around you, your family, and your beliefs.
